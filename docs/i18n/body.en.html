Elements & reactions

Three elements: fire, shock and acid. Two elements on one zombie trigger a reaction. After that the zombie has a 3 s reaction cooldown.

Fire + acid = Explosion

+=
  • Explosion: 50% of your last hit's damage (at least 60) to the target and every zombie within 4 m. It doesn't hurt you.

Fire + shock = Confusion

+=
  • 5 s: the zombie is 60% slower, takes more damage (−30 resistance) and is briefly stunned.

Shock + acid = Miasma

+=
  • 6 s: the zombie's max health drops by 20%, and current health drops with it. Plus bleeding.

Where elements come from

  • Fire: Incendiary mod, molotov, flaming arrows, Splinter at level 5, legendaries Dragon's Breath, Storm Fist, Artemis.
  • Shock: Tesla mod, batons (Chain Lightning), legendaries Thunderclap, Storm Fist.
  • Acid: Corrosive mod, legendary Hornet.
  • Bleeding from your hits (knives, serrated blade, barbed wire, Ripper): 5% of the last hit's damage per second (at least 5).
  • The vanilla Burning Shaft mod now ignites with a 5–30% chance instead of 10–60%: its fire also scales with hit damage now.

Affixes

Weapons from loot, zombies and traders get random affixes — stat bonuses, like in Borderlands. Crafted weapons have none. The higher the quality, the more affixes and the stronger they are.

How many affixes

  • Quality 1: no affixes.
  • Quality 2–3: up to 1 (50% chance).
  • Quality 4–5: up to 2 (75% / 35%).
  • Quality 6: up to 3 (100% / 60% / 30%), values at max.
  • A weapon never has the same affix twice. After combining two guns, extra affixes are removed.
  • Affixes show at the top of the item tooltip, and the icon has a star badge.

Firearms, bows, rocket launcher

Values: quality 2 → quality 6
  • Fire rate +5% → +25%
  • Spread −10% → −40%
  • Range +10% → +60%
  • Damage +5% → +35%
  • Reload speed +10% → +40%
  • Pellets (shotguns) +10% → +35%
  • Second bullet (pistols, rifles, assault rifles, quality 6 only): 2 bullets per shot, each −40% damage
  • Headshot damage +10% → +60%
  • Magazine size +10% → +60% (not on bows and crossbows)
  • Movement speed with the weapon in hand +2% → +10%
  • Recoil −10% → −50%

Elite zombies & Eridium

Sometimes a zombie spawns as an elite: its name with modifiers floats above its head, like in Borderlands. It's tougher, but always drops a weapon with affixes and Eridium, and Eridium lets you perfect your gun.

Elites

2% chance early → 5% late game · ★ = 1 modifier, ★★ after gamestage 100, ★★★ (25% of them) after gamestage 200
  • Twice the health (change it with aposelite health N or health in Settings.xml). Stars above the name: ★ yellow, ★★ orange, ★★★ red. Visible up to 40 m, not through walls.

Eridium

Purple crystal · stacks to 500 · can't be sold to traders
  • Sources: elites; scrapping weapons with affixes (2 per affix, ×2 at quality 6); scrapping a legendary (50). Eridium arrives when scrapping has finished (cancelling in the queue gives nothing). In a closed workstation Eridium waits in the output slots along with the materials.
  • Eridium Forge — a separate workstation (recipe at a regular workbench). Hold a weapon, open the forge, and each affix gets buttons:
  • Reroll (10): this affix gets a new type and value.
  • Lock (25): this affix no longer changes. A weapon can have one locked affix. Unlock is free.
  • Add (20): a new affix in an empty row, if the quality allows more.
  • Scrap: turns the weapon into Eridium.
  • Legendary of your choice (300): any of the ten, quality 6.
  • Sharpening ("Weapon" and "Apparel" tabs): +1 quality at a time, up to 10, for weapons (and legendaries) and any worn clothing with stats that grow with quality. Up to 6 — always succeeds (5×N Eridium and forged steel). 7–10 — 35→50, success 65%→30%, failure breaks the item (you get back 30% of the scrap value). "Safe" (×5): failure only lowers quality by 1, not below 6. Each level above 6 gives as much as a quality step in vanilla.

Weapon mastery

Each weapon (any quality: a quality 2 and quality 6 machete are the same) levels up from kills while it's in your hands. 10 levels = 10 points in a tree of 20 skills, so you can only take half. Alt+K — tree window for the weapon in hand; or in the inventory click a weapon → the third button next to "Stats / Description".

How it works

  • Levels: 4 / 12 / 24 / 40 / 60 / 90 / 120 / 160 / 210 / 260 kills. An elite counts as 5.
  • Tree: three branches going up from the root; each skill unlocks only next to a learned one. Mid-branch is a strong node, at the top a keystone (bold). Bridges connect the branches.
  • Reaching one branch's keystone takes 6 points, so you can't finish two branches.
  • The tree layout is shared per weapon class, points are separate for each weapon.
  • Reset: 5 Eridium per spent point, only within 5 m of the Eridium Forge.
  • "Crit" — chance to double a hit; "Crit power" makes it even bigger. Elements and crowd control — chance per hit.

Perks & attributes

Every perk has its own trait at max level, and every attribute at level 10. Vanilla effects that duplicated the mod's traits have been replaced. The level description in game has a yellow AposMod line with exact numbers.

Attributes — level 10

  • Perception — Executioner: rifles, spears and explosives deal +100% damage to targets below 30% health.
  • Strength — Overkill: excess damage from a killing blow with a shotgun, club or sledgehammer carries over to the nearest zombie within 4 m.
  • Fortitude — Iron Body: no bleeding, no infection, and an existing infection is cured.
  • Agility — Adrenaline: kills with a pistol, bow or knife give +10% run speed for 15 s, up to 3 stacks.
  • Intellect — Charged Chain Lightning: each kill gives a lightning charge. At 10 charges your next hit with any weapon releases Chain Lightning around you that also deals damage: 4 s of shock and 200 damage to every zombie within 8 m.

Perception — level 5

  • Flurry of Perception — Frenzy: a spear kill gives +25% attack speed for 5 s.
  • Demolitions Expert — Chain Reaction: a zombie killed by your explosives explodes itself: 250 damage within 4 m.
  • Penetrator — Shrapnel: armor-piercing rounds deal 40% of the shot's damage to everyone within 3 m of the target.
  • Infiltrator — Ghost: while crouched you're twice as hard to spot, hits on a zombie that can't see you deal ×2, and mines and traps don't hurt you.
  • Animal Tracker — Hunter's Mark: hitting an animal puts a Mark on it.
  • Treasure Hunter — X Marks the Spot: the treasure search circle shrinks almost to a point, and the chest holds a quality 5–6 weapon with affixes.
  • Salvage Operations — Junk Genius: salvage tools wear out 90% slower.
  • Perception Mastery — Lucky Shot: 10% chance that a hit with a Perception weapon deals +100% damage.

Strength — level 5

Armor sets & wardrobe

Combat sets are now tied to playstyles: pieces give bonuses on one theme, and the full set boosts a mod trait. Piece numbers grow with quality, as in vanilla, and further up to 10 through sharpening. Percentage full-set bonuses at quality 7–10 grow by 5% for each level above 6 (the worst piece counts).

Combat sets

  • Bruiser (was Biker) — clubs and sledgehammers: damage, cheaper power attacks, HP, running. Set: Earthquake reaches 1 m farther, Home Run knocks back farther, Rage holds 1 more stack.
  • Gunslinger (was Enforcer) — pistols: accuracy, damage, reload, running. Set: Headhunter after 4 headshots instead of 5 and +30% damage while it's active.
  • Ranger — rifles: recoil, HP, damage, stamina. Set: Ricochet reaches 3 m farther and is 20% stronger.
  • Commando — assault rifles: magazine, recoil, damage, running. Set: Suppression slows 20% more, every 3rd hit returns a round.
  • Hunter (was Farmer) — bows and crossbows: damage, stealth, draw and reload speed, running. Set: Splinter +2 splinters, +20% to marked zombies.
  • Preacher — elements: fire and shock resistance, HP, gloves raise the chance of elemental mods. Set: fire, shock, acid and bleeding +50%, reactions recharge in 1.5 s.
  • Nerd — batons and robots. Set: Chain Lightning reaches 1 m farther, lightning charges build up twice as fast.
  • Assassin and Raider — as in vanilla, plus: Assassin +2 Bloodsucker stacks and ×1.5 to zombies that can't see you; Raider +2 Counter Punch charges and −15% damage taken at full charges.

Utility sets

  • Lumberjack, Miner, Scavenger, Rogue, Athletic — as in vanilla.
  • Nomad — plus −50% fuel and +10% vehicle speed (moved over from Biker).
  • Primitive — got the farming bonuses (seeds, harvest).
  • DLC sets — cosmetic, no bonuses.

Wardrobe — 3 outfits

  • Alt+1 / Alt+2 / Alt+3 — change into an outfit in 1.5 s. Your current clothes go into the wardrobe and don't take backpack space.
  • Alt+0 — wardrobe window with three tabs: what's in each outfit, "Put on" and "Take to backpack" buttons.
  • How to fill it: put on the first set, press Alt+2, put on the second set from the backpack, press Alt+3 and put on the third.
  • The wardrobe is saved with the world and isn't dropped on death.

Stealth

  • Zombies in POIs that used to rush out in a wave after a trigger or attack right away now spawn asleep in their spots as you approach. Triggers (doors, loot, walls) don't wake them. Zombies spawn in advance: 12 m away and from the neighboring floor (the base game uses 8 m and only your floor), so they don't pop in right in front of you.
  • They wake up from noise or when they see you, so you can sneak up on them.
  • To turn off: <Stealth rework="false"/> in Settings.xml and restart the game.
