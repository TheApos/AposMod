Weapon mastery 2.0

Kills with a weapon level up its whole class (all pistols and SMGs share one level), up to 25. Every level is a point for that class's skill tree. Pick a class below and click a skill to read it; the planner lets you try a build before you spend points in the game.

How it works

  • Levels: 1-25 per weapon class, from kills with any weapon of that class. Saved with your game.
  • Class trait: every class has its own mechanic from level 0 (Headhunter, Bloodsucker, Earthquake and so on).
  • Branches: two branches per class. A tier opens after 0 / 3 / 6 / 9 / 12 points lower in the same branch; the top tier is a capstone with a downside.
  • Hybrids: skills between the branches need 5 points in each branch (the big one 8).
  • Window: Alt+K, or the mastery button in the item info panel.
  • Reset: near the Eridium Forge: one skill for 20 Eridium, the whole tree for 250.

HUD statuses

Everything the mod shows on the screen: arcs at the crosshair, rows of icons, statuses over zombies, labels over damage numbers and the reaction feed. Pick a weapon and click any status on the screen or in the list to see what it means and where it comes from.

Elements & reactions

Three elements: fire, shock and acid. Two elements on one zombie trigger a reaction. After that the zombie has a 3 s reaction cooldown.

Fire + acid = Explosion

+=
  • Explosion: 50% of your last hit's damage (at least 60) to the target and every zombie within 4 m. It doesn't hurt you.

Fire + shock = Confusion

+=
  • 5 s: the zombie is 60% slower, takes more damage (−30 resistance) and is briefly stunned.

Shock + acid = Miasma

+=
  • 6 s: the zombie's max health drops by 20%, and current health drops with it. Plus bleeding.

Where elements come from

  • Fire: Incendiary mod, molotov, flaming arrows, legendaries Dragon's Breath, Storm Fist, Artemis.
  • Shock: Tesla mod, batons (Chain Lightning), legendaries Thunderclap, Storm Fist.
  • Acid: Corrosive mod, legendary Hornet.
  • Bleeding from your hits (knives, serrated blade, barbed wire, Ripper): 5% of the last hit's damage per second (at least 5).
  • The vanilla Burning Shaft mod now ignites with a 5–30% chance instead of 10–60%: its fire also scales with hit damage now.

Affixes

Weapons from loot, zombies and traders get random affixes — stat bonuses, like in Borderlands. Crafted weapons have none. The higher the quality, the more affixes and the stronger they are.

How many affixes

  • Quality 1: no affixes.
  • Quality 2–3: up to 1 (50% chance).
  • Quality 4–5: up to 2 (75% / 35%).
  • Quality 6: up to 3 (100% / 60% / 30%); values roll in the top range (e.g. damage +25…35%).
  • A weapon never has the same affix twice. After combining two guns, extra affixes are removed; the Combine Station shows the result's affixes and which ones will be removed before you press Combine.
  • Affixes show at the top of the item tooltip, and the icon has a star badge.

Firearms, bows, rocket launcher

Values: quality 2 → quality 6
  • Fire rate +5% → +25%
  • Spread −10% → −40%
  • Range +10% → +60%
  • Damage +5% → +35%
  • Reload speed +10% → +40%
  • Pellets (shotguns) +10% → +35%
  • Second bullet (any gun except shotguns, quality 6 only): 2 bullets per shot, each −40% damage
  • Headshot damage +10% → +60%
  • Magazine size +10% → +60% (not on bows and crossbows)
  • Movement speed with the weapon in hand +2% → +10%
  • Recoil −10% → −50%

Melee

Values: quality 2 → quality 6
  • Damage +5% → +35%
  • Attack speed +4% → +20%
  • Stamina per hit −10% → −40%
  • Headshot damage +10% → +60%
  • Attack range +5% → +20%
  • Movement speed +2% → +10%
  • Life steal 1% → 6% of damage dealt
  • Dismember chance +5% → +30%
  • Durability +20% → +80%
  • Block damage +10% → +60%

Elite zombies & Eridium

Sometimes a zombie spawns as an elite: its name with modifiers floats above its head, like in Borderlands. It's tougher, but always drops a weapon with affixes and Eridium, and Eridium lets you perfect your gun.

Elites

2% chance early → 5% late game · ★ = 1 modifier, ★★ after gamestage 100, ★★★ (25% of them) after gamestage 200
  • Stronger with every star: ★ x3 health and x2 damage, ★★ x6 and x4, ★★★ x12 and x6 (change with aposelite health 3 6 12 / aposelite damage 2 4 6 or health1-3 / damage1-3 in Settings.xml). When an elite first comes into view, a banner names it and gives a tip for each of its modifiers (aposelite announce off turns it off). Stars above the name: ★ yellow, ★★ orange, ★★★ red. Visible up to 40 m, not through walls.
  • Loot on the ground glows and has a beam going up, like in Borderlands, so you see it from afar: Eridium purple, weapons with affixes gold, legendaries orange. Optionally also white above vanilla zombie loot bags: aposbags on or <LootBeam bags="true"/> in Settings.xml (off by default: lots of beams on blood moons).
  • Reward: ★ — weapon with affixes, quality 3–5, and 3–6 Eridium; ★★ — quality 5–6, 8–15 Eridium, 2% legendary; ★★★ — quality 6, 20–30 Eridium, 10% legendary. For weapon mastery an elite counts as 5 kills. Eridium always drops; the weapon drops by biome, more often where elites are tougher: pine forest 10%, burnt forest 20%, desert 30%, snow 40%, wasteland 50% (other biomes 100%, weaponDrop). Change it in Settings.xml (dropPineForest, dropBurntForest, dropDesert, dropSnow, dropWasteland, 0–100) or in game: aposelite drop wasteland N for one biome, aposelite drop N for all. Elites drop only player weapons (not the NPC-only guns of NPC mods); aposelite weapons lists them, and weaponExclude in Settings.xml removes some (comma-separated item names or name starts, e.g. gunExplosives,meleeToolAxe).

Eridium

Purple crystal · stacks to 500 · can't be sold to traders
  • Sources: elites; scrapping weapons with affixes (2 per affix, ×2 at quality 6); scrapping a legendary (50). Eridium arrives when scrapping has finished (cancelling in the queue gives nothing). In a closed workstation Eridium waits in the output slots along with the materials.
  • Eridium Forge — a separate workstation (recipe at a regular workbench). Hold a weapon, open the forge, and each affix gets buttons:
  • Reroll (10): this affix gets a new type and value.
  • Lock (25): this affix no longer changes. A weapon can have one locked affix. Unlock is free.
  • Add (20): a new affix in an empty row, if the quality allows more.
  • Scrap: turns the weapon into Eridium.
  • Legendary of your choice (300): any of the ten, quality 6.
  • Sharpening ("Weapon" and "Apparel" tabs): +1 quality at a time, up to 10, for weapons (and legendaries) and any worn clothing with stats that grow with quality. Up to 6 — always succeeds (5×N Eridium and forged steel). 7–10 — 35→50, success 65%→30%, failure breaks the item (you get back 30% of the scrap value). "Safe" (×5): failure only lowers quality by 1, not below 6. Levels above 6 grow like a hill: each one adds more than the one before. Weapon damage and armor resistances get 3, 6, 9 and 12 vanilla quality steps more at levels 7, 8, 9 and 10 (a pistol, +5% per quality step: +25% at 6, then +40% / +70% / +115% / +175% damage). Durability and other bonuses grow 1, 2, 3, 4 steps; mod slots +1 at level 10.

Perks & attributes

Every perk has its own trait at max level, and every attribute at level 10. Vanilla effects that duplicated the mod's traits have been replaced. The level description in game has a yellow AposMod line with exact numbers.

Attributes — level 10

  • Perception — Executioner: rifles, spears and explosives deal +100% damage to targets below 30% health.
  • Strength — Overkill: excess damage from a killing blow with a shotgun, club or sledgehammer carries over to the nearest zombie within 4 m.
  • Fortitude — Iron Body: no bleeding, no infection, and an existing infection is cured.
  • Agility — Adrenaline: kills with a pistol, bow or knife give +10% run speed and +5% damage for 15 s, up to 3 stacks.
  • Intellect — Charged Chain Lightning: each kill gives a lightning charge. At 10 charges your next hit with any weapon releases Chain Lightning around you that also deals damage: 4 s of shock and 200 damage (or half of that hit if it's more) to every zombie within 8 m.

Perception — level 5

  • Flurry of Perception — Frenzy: a spear kill gives +15% attack speed for 5 s.
  • Demolitions Expert — Chain Reaction: a zombie killed by your explosives explodes itself: 250 damage within 4 m, or half of the killing hit if that's more.
  • Penetrator — Shrapnel: armor-piercing rounds deal 40% of the shot's damage to everyone within 3 m of the target.
  • Infiltrator — Ghost: while crouched you're twice as hard to spot, hits on a zombie that can't see you deal ×2, mines don't hurt you and traps deal 25% less.
  • Animal Tracker — Hunter's Mark: hitting an animal puts a Mark on it.
  • Treasure Hunter — X Marks the Spot: the treasure search circle shrinks almost to a point, and the chest holds a quality 5–6 weapon with affixes.
  • Salvage Operations — Junk Genius: salvage tools wear out 90% slower.
  • Perception Mastery — Lucky Shot: 15% chance that a hit with a Perception weapon deals +100% damage.

Strength — level 5

  • Flurry of Strength — Frenzy: a club or sledgehammer kill gives +15% attack speed for 5 s.
  • Pack Mule — Beast of Burden: being encumbered no longer slows you.
  • Strength Mastery — Crippling Blows: a club or sledgehammer power attack breaks the zombie's legs, and it crawls.
  • Miner 69'er — Tunnel Rat: mining tools use 50% less stamina.
  • Mother Lode — Jackpot: hitting ore or wood has a 5% chance to give ×5 harvest for 2 s.
  • Junk Miner — Scrap King: +50% from junk piles and salvaging.

Fortitude — level 5

  • Flurry of Fortitude — Frenzy: a knuckles kill gives +15% attack speed for 5 s.
  • The Huntsman — Butcher: harvesting a carcass heals 30 HP.
  • Pain Tolerance — Unbreakable: below 25% health you take 45% less damage.
  • Fortitude Mastery — Second Wind: once every 5 min, when your health drops below 20%, you instantly restore 70%.
  • Siphoning Strikes (all levels): instead of the vanilla 2–10 HP per kill — life steal: every melee hit heals 4/8/12/16/20% of damage dealt (all life steal together — affix, mastery, this perk — is capped at 30%). Vampire (5): a melee kill gives +50 max health for 15 s (doesn't heal — fill it with life steal). Each kill during that time adds another +50 and restarts the 15 s — no cap. When the buff ends, the bonus is gone.
  • Healing Factor: natural regeneration 0.2 / 0.4 / 0.6 / 1 / 1.5 HP/s by level (vanilla 0.01–0.16). Regeneration (level 5): 6 s out of combat and you regenerate 4 HP/s more.
  • Slow Metabolism — Iron Stomach: no food poisoning, no dysentery (you can drink murky water), and food and water never drop below 25%.
  • Rule 1: Cardio — Second Lungs: when stamina runs out you instantly get +50% (once per 60 s).

Agility — level 5

  • Flurry of Agility — Frenzy: a knife kill gives +15% attack speed for 5 s.
  • Run and Gun — Gun Kata: hip fire on the move has no spread and +30% damage.
  • Hard Target — Blur: while running you take 45% less damage (with Unbreakable — 90% in total).
  • Parkour — Cat Landing: falls deal 99% less damage.
  • Agility Mastery — Evasion: after taking a hit you run 30% faster for 5 s.

Hunter in the Dark: at night and in dark places you sense zombies within 20 m through walls — a dim red glow that pulses like a heartbeat (sleepers — pale blue). Intellect — level 5

  • Electrocutioner — Overcharge: any of your weapons deals +25% damage to shocked zombies.
  • Better Barter — Black Market: after each stock refresh there's a 25% chance a trader has a legendary.

General perks — max level

  • Light Armor 4 — Nimble: +15% movement speed.
  • Medium Armor 4 — Balanced: attacks use 20% less stamina.
  • Heavy Armor 4 — Fortress: you can't be stunned or knocked down.
  • Master Chef 3 — Gourmet: food and drink effects last three times longer.
  • Living Off the Land 3 — Green Thumb: harvesting crops has a 50% chance to return a seed.
  • Lock Picking 3 — Safecracker: lockpicks almost never break.

Perks changed by Weapon mastery 2.0

  • Grand Slam: hidden, its points are given back. Rage is a skill of the club tree.
  • Pummel Pete: the bonus to stunned zombies, the hit combo and the power-hit knockdown are removed (the club tree has Kick Them While Down, Concussion and Batting Streak).
  • Skull Crusher: the power-hit knockdowns are removed (the sledgehammer trait Earthquake does it).
  • The Brawler: the power-hit stun and the head knockdown are removed (Counter Punch knocks down).
  • Deep Cuts: normal knife hits no longer bleed (the knife skill Deep Cuts does it); power hits keep the vanilla wounds.
  • Gunslinger 3–5: the +100% shot after a series of hits is removed (Headhunter and Firing Squad do it).
  • Boomstick: no mod bonus any more; Pump Action is a skill of the shotgun tree.
  • Everything else these perks do in the base game still works.

Intellect Mastery 4–5

  • 4 — Field Repair: all weapons, tools and armor wear out 30% slower.
  • 5 — Inventor: crafted weapons get random affixes, like looted weapons of the same quality. Everything crafted comes out one quality level above the quality you pick in the recipe (pick 5, get 6); if you pick 6, weapons, tools and armor come out at quality 6, forge tier 7.
  • The old baton bonuses (charge in 2 hits, +300% shock damage) are removed.

Armor sets & wardrobe

Combat sets are now tied to playstyles: pieces give bonuses on one theme, and the full set boosts a mod trait. Piece numbers grow with quality, as in vanilla, and further up to 10 through sharpening. Percentage full-set bonuses at quality 7–10 grow by 5% for each level above 6 (the worst piece counts).

Combat sets

  • Bruiser (was Biker) — clubs and sledgehammers: damage, cheaper power attacks, HP, running. Set: Earthquake reaches 1 m farther, Home Run knocks back farther, Rage holds 1 more stack.
  • Gunslinger (was Enforcer) — pistols: accuracy, damage, reload, running. Set: Headhunter after 4 headshots instead of 5 and +30% damage while it's active.
  • Ranger — rifles: recoil, HP, damage, stamina. Set: Ricochet reaches 3 m farther and is 20% stronger.
  • Commando — assault rifles: magazine, recoil, damage, running. Set: Suppression slows 20% more, every 3rd hit returns a round.
  • Hunter (was Farmer) — bows and crossbows: damage, stealth, draw and reload speed, running. Set: Splinter +2 splinters, +20% to marked zombies.
  • Preacher — elements: fire and shock resistance, HP, gloves raise the chance of elemental mods. Set: fire, shock, acid and bleeding +50%, reactions recharge in 1.5 s.
  • Nerd — batons and robots. Set: Chain Lightning reaches 1 m farther, lightning charges build up twice as fast.
  • Assassin and Raider — as in vanilla, plus: Assassin +2 Bloodsucker stacks and ×1.5 to zombies that can't see you; Raider +2 Counter Punch charges and −15% damage taken at full charges.

Utility sets

  • Lumberjack, Miner, Scavenger, Rogue, Athletic — as in vanilla.
  • Nomad — plus −50% fuel and +10% vehicle speed (moved over from Biker).
  • Primitive — got the farming bonuses (seeds, harvest).
  • DLC sets — cosmetic, no bonuses.

Wardrobe — 3 outfits

  • Alt+1 / Alt+2 / Alt+3 — change into an outfit in 1.5 s. Your current clothes go into the wardrobe and don't take backpack space. Only the 4 armor pieces change: clothing under the armor (it only gives cold and heat resistance), the backpack and biome badges stay on. The outfit number (I / II / III) is shown left of the toolbelt, and the character window has a Wardrobe button.
  • Alt+0 — wardrobe window with three tabs: icons and quality of each outfit's armor pieces, total armor and elemental resistance, the armor set and whether its bonus is active, "Put on" and "Take to backpack" buttons.

Gold name · trophy badge · always quality 6 · 4% in ★4 final chests, 10% in ★5 · Black Market

Each legendary has 3 fixed affixes at quality 6 max and its own trait, so it's always at least as good as the best weapon with random affixes.

  • Unkempt Harold (.44 Desert Vulture): 3 bullets per shot in a fan. Damage +35%, fire rate +25%, magazine +60%.
  • Lady Fist (9mm pistol): +50% damage, every hit heals 5 HP, a headshot 25 HP. Headshot damage +60%, spread −40%, reload +40%.
  • Dragon's Breath (pump shotgun): +20%, every hit ignites. Pellets +35%, damage +35%, reload +40%.
  • The Ripper (machete): +40%, every hit causes bleeding, +50% more to targets already bleeding; a kill heals 20 HP. Damage +35%, attack speed +20%, life steal 6% of damage.
  • Thunderclap (steel sledgehammer): +25%, a power attack shocks everyone within 4 m. Each kill gives a storm charge; at 3 charges a power attack calls down lightning: 150 damage and 3 s of shock to everyone within 6 m. Damage +35%, stamina per hit −40%, headshot damage +60%.
  • Hornet (SMG-5): +40% damage, hits have a 50% chance to corrode with acid, +25% more against corroded targets. Magazine +60%, fire rate +25%, recoil −50%.

  • Big Boom (rocket launcher): 50% chance the next rocket is free. Damage +35%, reload +40%, range +60%.
  • Predator (sniper rifle): +25%, every headshot ricochets within 15 m: 100 damage and 2 s of stun. Headshot damage +60%, range +60%, recoil −50%.

Stealth

  • Zombies in POIs that used to rush out in a wave after a trigger or attack right away now spawn asleep in their spots as you approach. Triggers (doors, loot, walls) don't wake them. Zombies spawn in advance: 12 m away and from the neighboring floor (the base game uses 8 m and only your floor), so they don't pop in right in front of you.
  • They wake up from noise or when they see you, so you can sneak up on them.
  • To turn off: <Stealth rework="false"/> in Settings.xml and restart the game. Already visited POIs return to vanilla too (since 1.6.11).

Combat HUD — aposhud

  • The mod's statuses are not in the vanilla buff column: the combat HUD shows them by the crosshair, by the toolbelt and above zombies. Each one is explained in HUD statuses.
  • Every counter is also a line in the Status Effects window, with its description.
  • aposhud on / off, aposhud size 0.6–1.6, aposhud pos below|right|left.
