## Name (letters, numbers, spaces and _ ' ( ) . - only)
AposMod - Borderlands-style Weapon Traits and Loot

## Category
Gameplay

## Summary (short description)
Every weapon class gets its own Borderlands-style trait. Loot rolls random affixes, elite zombies (1-3 stars) drop Eridium for a forge that sharpens gear up to quality 10, weapons gain mastery and skill trees, perks get level-5 highlights, colored damage numbers. Needs -noeac (DLL).

[*][b]Perk and attribute highlights[/b] - every perk at max level and every attribute at level 10 gets its own feature (Rage, Shadow Strike, Hunter in the Dark, Vampire, Inventor...).

[*][b]Special arrows[/b] (acid, electric, serrated), bow auto-nock and an instant arrow wheel.
[*][b]Stealth rework[/b] - sleepers spawn asleep in place instead of rushing you on triggers.
